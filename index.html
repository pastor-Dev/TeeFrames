About

Hi, I'm Tee

I make short videos that look expensive without the expensive crew.

I shoot on a phone because it gets me closer, moves faster and never makes your guests nervous. Then I edit every frame myself, so the finished film has one point of view from start to end.

Based in Nigeria and available for shoots here. Remote editing for clients anywhere.

How I shoot
Camera
Phone at 4K, 24 or 30 fps, exposure and focus locked for steady shots.
Movement
Gimbal for smooth walk-throughs and tracking shots.
Sound
Lavalier mic for clean voice. Audio gets as much care as picture.
Light
Small LED panel and natural light, planned around the time of day.
Edit
Colour, sound and motion text finished in a full editing app.
